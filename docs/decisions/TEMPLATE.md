# NNNN — Title (imperative, states the decision, not the problem)

**Status:** accepted | superseded by NNNN | deprecated
**Decided:** YYYY-MM-DD (what settled it — the spike, issue, or PR). The date the choice was *taken*,
not the date the work merged. A rev that changes what was chosen moves it.
**Rev:** N — matches the rev in the index table, and the rev every citation of this ADR pins.

## Revisions

Newest first, one line per rev: what changed and the ticket that changed it. A rev that records a
supersession says so here — `superseded by ADR NNNN rev M`.

- **rev 1** — YYYY-MM-DD — first written (#N ticket name).

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraint, the bug, the requirement — whatever made "just pick
something" not good enough.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated as plainly as possible.

## Alternatives considered

At least one rejected option, and *why* it was rejected. An ADR with no rejected alternative is
just a changelog entry with a heading — if there isn't a real alternative to name, this doesn't
need an ADR; put the rationale in the commit message instead.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it makes harder, and anything it forecloses.
